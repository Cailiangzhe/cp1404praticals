## Estimates

Regarding the **estimates** that you did for practical tasks...

### How was your estimate accuracy usually?

My estimation accuracy was low to begin with, and I often underestimated the time it would take to complete a task, especially if it required debugging or refactoring.

### How did your estimate accuracy improve or change during the course of the subject?

As my understanding of task complexity deepened, I learned to allocate time more rationally, including reserving extra time to deal with potential problems, and the accuracy of my estimates improved significantly.

### What did you learn from doing these estimates?

I learned to break down large tasks into smaller parts for estimation, and realized that accounting for “unforeseen issues” is one of the keys to accurate estimation.
